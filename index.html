Fizouli Faradjev (F.E. Faradjev)

Professor & Corresponding Member of the Russian Academy of Natural Sciences (RANS)
Honored Scientist and Educator | Uppsala, Sweden

About

Physicist, researcher, and educator specializing in nanophotonics, semiconductor quantum-dimensional structures, and laser spectroscopy. Recognized for pioneering work on self-assembled quantum dots, including the first observation of extremely narrow photoluminescence and sharp emission at 1200 nm in InAsP/InP quantum dots.

Alongside solid-state physics, my recent work investigates art authentication, forensic science, antitrust litigation, and epistemic justice in art history.

Research Areas

  • Nanophotonics & Optoelectronics: Propagation of laser radiation in quantum-size semiconductor structures, telecommunications, and biophotonics.
  • Quantum Structures: Self-organizing quantum dots (InAsP/InP) and strained multiple quantum wells (GaInNAs/GaAs).
  • Spectroscopy & Epitaxy: Low-temperature photoluminescence (PL), laser spectroscopy, and low-pressure MOVPE.
  • Art History & Epistemology: Forensic art analysis, the catalogue raisonné system, and gatekeeping in the art market.
